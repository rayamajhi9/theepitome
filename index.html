Rooted in connection

A farm works best when nothing goes to waste.

The Epitome Integrated Organic Farm is building a practical, regenerative model for small farms in Nepal. We bring crops and free-ranging chickens together so nutrients return to the soil and the farm can rely less on costly outside inputs.

And a healthy food system doesn’t end at the farm gate. Through farm visits, learning experiences and closer farmer-to-consumer connections, we’re inviting our community to be part of the change.
